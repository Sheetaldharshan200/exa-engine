**Exasol Studio** is the graphical home for your Exasol databases: a schema browser, a SQL editor with performance insight, dashboards, a marketplace of add-ons, and an AI assistant — all speaking natively to Exasol.

It is one product with two doors, and this documentation covers both:

- **Desktop app** — the full experience, including managing a local **Exasol Personal** database on your machine.
- **`exa web`** — the same Studio served in your browser by the [exa engine](../exa/index.mdx), sharing the same connections. See [Web](../exa/usage/web.mdx).

<Callout type="info" title="Studio and exa are one stack">
Connections live in a registry both programs share (`~/.exasol/connections.json`, passwords in the OS credential store). Connect a database in either and it appears in both. The agent inside Studio is the exa engine itself.
</Callout>
